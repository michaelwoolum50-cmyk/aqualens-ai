### Project title
AquaLens AI — AI-Assisted Stream Health Assessment with a Human in the Loop

### Tagline
From streams to systems: explainable on-device AI turns citizen photos into verified One Health stream data — humans always decide.

### Description (Devpost "About")
Urban freshwater ecosystems are degrading, but monitoring is sparse. Citizen science can fill the
gap — yet citizen observations are inconsistent, jargon-heavy tools kill participation, and raw AI
verdicts aren't trustworthy without human judgment.

AquaLens AI is a mobile-first web app that closes this loop:

1. **Photograph** — citizen snaps their stream; on-device computer vision reads algae signals
   (green-pixel ratio + vegetation index) and turbidity (dark-pixel ratio + brightness). No photo
   ever leaves the phone.
2. **Observe** — six plain-language questions ground every AI signal with human observation.
3. **Verify** — an explainable weighted engine (algae .22, turbidity .20, litter .18, odor .15,
   biodiversity .15, flow .10) proposes findings with reasoning + confidence shown. The citizen
   confirms, adjusts, or rejects EACH finding. Nothing saves until every finding has a human verdict.
   Output: a 0–100 Stream Health Index.
4. **Share** — local trend dashboard plus one-tap **FHIR R4 Observation export** so researchers
   ingest citizen data into real health-data pipelines.

Single HTML file. Zero backend. Works offline. No dependencies, no cost to run or scale.

### Built with
HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (Canvas computer vision), FHIR R4, localStorage
